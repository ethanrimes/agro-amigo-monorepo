# Source coverage and research

Verified for the September 2026 demonstration. Every integrated dataset has an in-app source link, date/period, immutable Azure document and publisher URL. Source links below point to the publisher; numerical extraction manifests and exact download URLs are in `pipelines/planning`.

Copernicus terrain/vegetation imagery and automated pest-risk models were not implemented. No plot polygon, remote crop diagnosis, guaranteed yield, precise irrigation quantity, live buyer verification, payment reputation or commercial offer marketplace is implied. The source library and methods expose these distinctions in ordinary Spanish.

## Statistical and product judgment

The crop shortlist combines observed municipal cultivation and compatible mapped suitability. It does not establish profitability or recommend a crop for a precise parcel. Cost and yield scenarios are separately editable. Only comparable physical product states and markets are used for seasonal ratios; missing history yields an explicit insufficient-data state. Lower/central/upper outputs are scenarios with visible assumptions, not probabilities or financial forecasts.
